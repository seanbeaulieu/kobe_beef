    About

    This map shows restaurants in Kobe city that appear on the official registry of designated Kobe Beef stores (神戸肉流通推進協議会 指定登録店), maintained by the Kobe Beef Marketing & Distribution Promotion Association.

    Registered restaurants are permitted to serve and advertise genuine Kobe Beef, and display a registration certificate and a bronze bull statue in the store.

    Data

    • Restaurant list: kobe-niku.jp
    • Locations: the registry where provided, otherwise geocoded with the GSI address search (block-level accuracy).
    • Hours: OpenStreetMap contributors, where available, plus manual curation.
    • Price & rating: manually curated; many entries are still unknown.

    Not affiliated with the Kobe Beef Association. Always confirm hours and prices with the restaurant before visiting.
