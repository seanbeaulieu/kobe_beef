    神戸ビーフ · About

    The real thing, mapped.

    Every restaurant in Kobe on the official registry of designated Kobe Beef stores, the places allowed to serve and advertise genuine Kobe beef.

    What makes it Kobe beef

    Plenty of menus say “Wagyu”. Only beef that clears every one of these bars may be called Kobe beef (神戸牛 / 神戸ビーフ):

    1. Tajima lineagePurebred Tajima cattle from Hyogo seed stock, raised on farms in Hyogo Prefecture registered with the Association.
    2. Heifer or steerBulls and breeding cows don't qualify.
    3. Quality score 4 or 5And a yield score of A or B under the Japanese grading system.
    4. Marbling BMS 6 or higherOn the 12-point Beef Marbling Standard.
    5. Carcass under 500 kg270–499.9 kg for heifers, 300–499.9 kg for steers.

    Certified beef carries the Nojigiku (wild chrysanthemum) stamp and a 10-digit ID that traces it back to the individual animal.

    The registry

    The Kobe Beef Marketing & Distribution Promotion Association (神戸肉流通推進協議会) registers the restaurants and shops that buy certified Kobe beef. Designated stores display a registration certificate and a bronze bull statue. The number on each listing here is that registration number.
