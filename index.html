    Where to eat Kobe beef in Kobe

    This map lists every restaurant in Kobe city on the register of designated Kobe Beef stores: the places that buy certified carcasses and are allowed to call what they serve Kobe beef.

    What makes it Kobe beef

    Plenty of menus say “Wagyu”. Only beef that clears every one of these bars may be called Kobe beef (神戸牛 / 神戸ビーフ):

    1. Tajima lineagePurebred Tajima cattle from Hyogo seed stock, raised on farms in Hyogo Prefecture registered with the Association, and processed in Hyogo.
    2. Heifer or steerAt least 28 months old. Bulls and cows that have calved don't qualify.
    3. Quality score 4 or 5And a yield score of A or B under the Japanese grading system.
    4. Marbling BMS 6 or higherOn the 12-point Beef Marbling Standard.
    5. Carcass under 500 kg270–499.9 kg for heifers, 300–499.9 kg for steers.

    Certified carcasses get the association's chrysanthemum (nojigiku) stamp and a certificate of authenticity, and the 10-digit ID traces the beef back to the individual animal.

    How to tell real Kobe beef from fake

    The registry

    The Kobe Beef Marketing & Distribution Promotion Association (神戸肉流通推進協議会) registers the restaurants and shops that buy certified Kobe beef. Designated stores display a registration certificate and a bronze statuette of a Tajima cow. The number on each listing here is that registration number.
